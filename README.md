# City Cooling — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

City Cooling Pte. Ltd., also operating as City Cooling Engineering, is a Singapore-based air-conditioning specialist serving residential and commercial customers since 2018.

The company provides islandwide aircon servicing, installation, chemical cleaning, repairs, troubleshooting, gas refilling, and preventive maintenance solutions.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating the company's services more effectively, strengthening customer confidence, and simplifying the enquiry process through a modern, professional, and service-focused digital experience.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Homepage Messaging and Value Proposition

**Previous Issue:** The original homepage opened with the incomplete message "STAY COOL WITH", which did not immediately establish a distinctive value proposition or clearly communicate the company's services and customer benefits.

**Redesign Improvement:** Strengthened the homepage introduction with clearer air-conditioning service messaging, relevant customer benefits, and more prominent enquiry actions. The improved content hierarchy helps visitors quickly understand what City Cooling offers and how to request assistance.

### 2. Clearer and More Consistent Trust Communication

**Previous Issue:** The original homepage presented multiple credibility claims, including customer reviews, technician certifications, and project statistics, without a sufficiently consistent hierarchy or clear supporting information.

**Redesign Improvement:** Reorganised trust-building content into a more structured presentation, prioritising the company's experience, service capabilities, and relevant customer reassurance. Credibility claims requiring verification are identified for confirmation rather than presented as independently established facts.

### 3. Improved Contact Information Consistency

**Previous Issue:** The original website displayed conflicting secondary WhatsApp numbers between the homepage footer and contact page. Inconsistent address formatting also created potential confusion for customers attempting to contact the company.

**Redesign Improvement:** Established a clearer contact-information structure with more accessible telephone, WhatsApp, and enquiry pathways. The redesign prioritises consistent presentation of business details, with conflicting WhatsApp information flagged for verification before publication.

## Design Improvements

The homepage redesign focuses on several targeted visual and usability enhancements:

- **Clearer Visual Hierarchy:** Improved the progression from the main service introduction to aircon solutions, credibility information, and customer enquiries.
- **Stronger Service Positioning:** Emphasised residential and commercial air-conditioning services through clearer messaging and content organisation.
- **Improved Service Discovery:** Presented key offerings such as general servicing, chemical cleaning, installation, repairs, and maintenance more accessibly.
- **More Cohesive Trust Presentation:** Organised experience, qualifications, reviews, and service-related information into a clearer structure.
- **Improved Contact Accessibility:** Made telephone, WhatsApp, and enquiry actions easier to locate throughout the customer journey.
- **Responsive User Experience:** Prioritised readable content, intuitive navigation, and convenient contact options across desktop and mobile devices.
- **Consistent Visual Presentation:** Focused on balanced spacing, readable typography, structured layouts, and a professional appearance suitable for an established air-conditioning service provider.

## Summary

The redesigned City Cooling homepage addresses three primary communication and usability challenges: unclear introductory messaging, inconsistent trust presentation, and conflicting contact information.

These improvements establish a clearer, more credible, and enquiry-focused website experience while reinforcing the company's air-conditioning expertise and islandwide service capabilities.
